# Global Malnutrition Trends: A Power BI Analysis (1983–2019)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes **global malnutrition trends** from **1983 to 2019**, focusing on children under five years of age.  
The dataset used for this project was sourced from **Kaggle**, which compiles historical malnutrition estimates for different countries and income groups.  

The analysis explores different forms of malnutrition such as:
- Severe wasting  
- Wasting  
- Stunting  
- Underweight  
- Overweight  

The study also examines correlations with **income classifications** (low, lower-middle, upper-middle, and high income) and special country categories like:
- Least Developed Countries (LDC)  
- Low-Income Food Deficient (LIFD)  
- Land-Locked Developing Countries (LLDC)  
- Small Island Developing States (SIDS)  

## Objectives
- Identify global patterns and trends in child malnutrition.  
- Understand how malnutrition correlates with **income levels** and **geographical categories**.  
- Highlight countries and regions most affected.  
- Provide **data-driven insights** to support policy-making and resource allocation.  

## Key Scenarios Analyzed
1. **Count of Under-5 Population (140)** – Sample size observations in dataset.  
2. **Sum of Survey Sample (11M)** – Total survey responses collected.  
3. **Sum of Underweight (2.08K)** – Cases of underweight children.  
4. **Stunting by Income Analysis** – Trend showing higher income = lower stunting.  
5. **Overweight by Country** – Distribution of overweight children globally.  
6. **Overweight vs. Underweight under Income Classification** – Ribbon chart analysis.  
7. **Sum of Income Classification** – Breakdown by income categories.

## Project Workflow
1. **Data Collection** – Dataset downloaded from Kaggle.  
2. **Data Connection** – Imported into Power BI.  
3. **Data Preparation** – Cleaning and transformation for analysis.  
4. **Visualizations** – Stacked bar charts, line charts, ribbon charts, etc.  
5. **Dashboard Design** – Responsive and interactive dashboard in Power BI.  
6. **Reporting** – Documentation of findings and insights.  
7. **Performance Testing** – Ensuring optimized visuals and filters.  
8. **Final Delivery** – Dashboard + Explanation video + Documentation.  

## Dataset
The repository includes the raw dataset files used in this analysis:
- `malnutrition-estimates.csv`  
- `country-wise-average.csv`  

These were sourced from Kaggle and form the basis for the Power BI dashboard.  

## Dashboard Highlights
- Interactive **filters** for income levels, countries, and malnutrition types.  
- **Comparative visuals** showing overweight vs. underweight by income.  
- **Trend lines** displaying improvements or worsening malnutrition over decades.  
- Insights into **most vulnerable regions** requiring urgent intervention.
